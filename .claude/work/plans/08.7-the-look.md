# 08.7: The look

> Historical: built and committed; see the roadmap for what followed.

**Goal:** Bava looks quiet and precise, in black and white, on every screen
and in both themes, with nothing it does changed.
**Specs:** `.claude/work/specs/look.md` decisions 1 to 12 (2026-10-11);
mockups (reference, not spec; deleted 2026-10-11, the spec holds every decision);
`.ai/rules/design-system.md` (tokens, no literals, wrapped components);
`.claude/work/specs/brand.md` (the mark, unchanged).
**File format impact:** none. Tags still save as `launch`; the `#` is drawn.
**UI impact:** tokens retuned in `_color.scss`, `_swatches.scss`,
`_radius.scss`, `_elevation.scss` and `_type.scss`; tokens added:
`--color-pending` (unsaved), `--font-label` and `--font-data` (both the mono
stack); one component added, `Keycaps` (a shortcut drawn as key boxes);
`Shell`, `TagChip`, `ContextMenu` and the canvas stage gain the four small
touches; every other change is a component's styles, through tokens.

## Constraints

- **Tokens only.** `no-literals.test.ts` keeps holding: no hex, `rgb()` or
  `px` outside `tokens/`. Dark mode never needs a file outside `tokens/`.
- **The palette, verbatim from the spec (decision 2):**

  | Role | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ground | `#ffffff` | `#0a0a0a` |
  | Chrome | `#fafafa` | `#101010` |
  | Sunken | `#f5f5f5` | `#151515` |
  | Raised | `#ffffff` | `#171717` |
  | Hairline | `#ebebeb` | `#1f1f1f` |
  | Strong line | `#dcdcdc` | `#2c2c2c` |
  | Ink, and the accent | `#0a0a0a` | `#f4f4f4` |
  | Secondary text | `#2b2b2b` | `#cfcfcf` |
  | Muted text | `#7a7a7a` | `#7c7c7c` |
  | Faint text, idle icons | `#a6a6a6` | `#575757` |
  | Wash | `#f0f0f0` | `#1e1e1e` |
  | Text highlight | `#e6e6e6` | `#333333` |

  Red for what deletes and green for saved are kept; neither is an accent.
- **No accent setting** (decision 3). **No coloured edge on one side only**
  (decision 4). **Dots on the canvas** (5). **Shape swatches stay coloured**
  (6). **Data and section labels in Geist Mono**, labels in tracked capitals,
  tabular figures where numbers line up (7). **Start screen on a plain
  ground** (8). **Tools on the left** (9). **Hairlines; shadows only on what
  floats; corners 4 to 6px on controls, 10px on windows and dialogs** (11).
- **Nothing a control does changes** (decision 12). Strings in `messages.ts`.

### Settled in this plan (say if you disagree)

The spec does not decide these; each follows from it:

1. **Token mapping.** `surface` is Ground, `surface-nav` Chrome,
   `surface-sunken` Sunken, `surface-raised` and `surface-overlay` Raised,
   `border-subtle` Hairline, `border-strong` Strong line, `accent` Ink,
   `accent-subtle` Wash, `text-primary` Ink. Two have no row in the table and
   are derived: `border-hairline` (fainter than Hairline: `#f3f3f3` /
   `#171717`) and `text-prose` (a step softer than Ink: `#1f1f1f` /
   `#dcdcdc`). Accent hover and pressed are a step lighter in light
   (`#2b2b2b`, `#3d3d3d`) and darker in dark (`#ffffff`, `#dcdcdc`).
   Selection is ink at 12% (light) and 20% (dark); the focus ring is ink with
   a 15% (light) and 25% (dark) halo.
2. **The canvas ground is Ground**, as in the mockups (today it is a sunken
   grey); its dots `#d4d4d4` / `#2e2e2e`. The unstyled shape fill becomes
   Sunken so a shape still stands off the ground.
3. **The canvas's own marks follow the accent:** selection outline and
   handles, the outline of the shape an arrow would attach to, and snapping
   guides are Ink (today blue, indigo and red).
4. **Kept as they are:** code syntax colours, the note fill, find's yellow
   matches, the diagram preview's colours (passed to D2, never used by the
   app's chrome). They carry meaning, not accent.
5. **Faint text** keeps the spec's `#a6a6a6` / `#575757`, used only where it
   is today (hints, idle icons, empty-state help), never for text a person
   must read to act.
6. **Position while dragging** reads `x 520 · y 320`: the moving
   selection's top-left in canvas units, rounded to whole numbers, drawn
   8px below the selection's bottom-left corner at any zoom, only while a
   move is under way. Not shown while resizing, rotating or drawing.
7. **The saved dot** is 6px, green when saved, amber (`--color-pending`:
   `#c98a12` / `#e0a93a`) while unsaved; the word beside it stays.

## As built

Bava is black and white: the palette from the spec in both themes, the
accent the ink (selection, focus, primary buttons, ticked boxes, Space
letters, the canvas's handles, attach outline and snapping guides), pure
greys, a white (near-black) canvas with its dots, and the shape swatches
kept with their dark fills deepened for the new ground. Section labels are
mono capitals, and sizes, dates, paths and counts are mono with tabular
figures. The title bar's state has a dot (green saved, amber unsaved); tags
read `#launch` (the `#` drawn, hidden from screen readers, never saved);
menu shortcuts are key boxes (`Keycaps`); a moving selection shows `x · y`
below it. Dialogs and windows round at 10px; a chosen segment is outlined,
not lifted.

**Deviations**

- **Every Space letter is now the ink**, as decision 2 lists "Space
  letters": the per-Space colours (a swatch chosen from the name) are gone
  from the start screen and the Space switcher.
- **No Escape test for the readout:** Bava has no Escape that cancels a
  move, so the readout clears on release only.
- **The readout is checked by unit tests and a picture**, not an
  integration test: the canvas draws it into its bitmap, where a browser
  test cannot read text. `readout.test.ts` covers the text and placement,
  `pointer.test.ts` that only a move reports what it carries, and the
  picture `canvas/shape--moving` shows it mid-drag (the snapping picture
  shows it too).
- **A page's h6** keeps the page's face with its own tracking
  (`--tracking-doc-h6`): it is the writer's text, not an app label.
- **The settings section's outline** in the pictures is the keyboard focus
  ring (the dialog opens with focus on it), not a selected-state outline;
  kept.
- **Callout colours, the quote's grey rule and the info callout's blue**
  are content, as the shape swatches are; kept.
- **A chosen row fills with `selection`** (ink at 12%, 20% in dark), a
  step above the Wash, which is its hover; the spec's "wash" is read as a
  full-row fill.
- **A callout has no border:** it keeps its kind's fill, and its icon
  carries the emphasis.
- **Corners are 3 to 6px on controls**, the existing small radius kept.
- `ContextMenu` does render under jsdom here, so its keys are unit-tested
  as well as pictured.
