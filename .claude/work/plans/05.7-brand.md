# 05.7: Brand

**Goal:** Put the panda mark into the product: app icons, title bar, the
files-pane empty state and About, following the v2 handover.

**Specs:**
- `.claude/work/specs/brand.md`: decisions made 2026-09-17 (colour rule, icon source)
- The Claude Design brand handover v3 (temporary, outside the repo; its rules are recorded in `.claude/work/specs/brand.md` and its art vendored)
- `.ai/rules/design-system.md`: inventory, tokens only, Ark wrapping
- `.claude/plan/roadmap.md`: Milestone 5.7

**File format impact:** none.

**UI impact:**
- Added: `Mark` (component), `AboutDialog` (component), tokens
  `--color-mark` and `--color-mark-tile`.
- Changed: `EmptyState` gains an optional mark; `Shell` title bar shows the
  mark; About moves off `ConfirmDialog`.

## Constraints

- **Paper on ink, always.** Mark `#e6e6e3`. On a light ground the mark sits in
  a `#131416` tile; on a dark ground it sits bare. Never the accent.
- One drawing at every size, unmodified. 20px floor for brand placements
  (About, empty states); system chrome (title bar) may use 16px.
- Wordmark: Geist Medium, lowercase `bava`. Tracking −0.4px applies at 22px
  and above only.
- Tokens only: `no-literals.test.ts` fails on any hex, rgb or px outside
  `tokens/`. The two new colour tokens are recorded additions beyond the
  design sheet, like `text-prose`.
- The mark is inlined as SVG with `fill="currentColor"`, never loaded by URL,
  so colour comes from the token.
- Icon PNGs are designer exports, committed as delivered, not generated.
- `actool` is not installed: `Assets.car` cannot be rebuilt here.
