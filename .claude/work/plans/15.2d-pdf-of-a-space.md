# 15.2d: A folder or the Space as one PDF

**Goal:** Export Space offers PDF beside the Markdown zip: the whole Space
or a folder as one PDF, a contents page with page numbers first, then each
page's Document and its Canvas, with bookmarks.
**Specs:** `.claude/work/specs/export.md` decisions 1 to 5, 8 to 14
(2026-10-11); `.claude/work/specs/documents.md` decision 48;
`.claude/work/plans/15.2c-pdf-of-a-page.md` (As built); `docs/ipc.md`;
`.ai/rules/design-system.md`, `ipc.md`, `editors.md`, `testing.md`.
**File format impact:** none.
**UI impact:** reused: `SpaceExportDialog` (gains Format and the PDF
options), `PdfPreview`, `NumberField`, `Segments`, `Select`, `Toggle`,
`Progress`; added: `PdfOptions` (the PDF settings, taken out of
`PageExportDialog` so both dialogs share them). Strings in `messages.ts`.

## Constraints

- **One combined PDF** (decision 12): every page of the Space or folder in
  one file.
- **Contents first** (decision 11): each page's name and the sheet it
  starts on, under its folder's name, in the Files tree's order;
  bookmarks give the same jumps.
- **Each page brings its Document, then its Canvas if it holds
  something** (decision 13), the Canvas on a sheet of its own as in 15.2c.
- **A page that cannot be read or printed stops the export** (decision
  14): nothing is written, and Bava says which page and why.
- Paper, margins, theme and page numbers as in 15.2c (decisions 3, 4, 8,
  9); the printing webview reaches nothing.

### Settled in this plan (say if you disagree)

1. **The contents page counts as sheet 1** when numbers are on, and the
   numbers it lists are the ones printed on the sheets.
2. **Folders are headings in the contents and parent bookmarks**: a
   folder's bookmark opens its first page; pages sit under it. Exporting a
   folder, the folder's own name is the PDF's title, its pages and
   sub-folders below.
3. **The contents page is titled with the Space's or folder's name** and
   drawn in Bava's font in the chosen theme.
4. **The preview shows the first page's first sheet** as it will print;
   the contents page is made only while printing, once every page's sheet
   count is known.
5. **The pages are drawn when Export is pressed**, one after another, the
   dialog saying "Drawing 3 of 12"; Cancel stops it before printing
   starts. Printing then runs as one step ("Printing the PDF"); it cannot be
   stopped, as the system's printing has no cancel.
6. **The open page is saved first**, as for the zip.
7. **Pages in a folder Bava cannot list are not in the PDF**, as they are
   not in the Files tree; a page listed but unreadable stops the export
   (decision 14).

## As built (2026-10-11)

Export Space offers Format, PDF or Markdown zip, What as before, and the
shared `PdfOptions`; the preview is the first page's first sheet. Export
PDF asks where, draws each page in turn ("Drawing 3 of 12", Cancel stops
it), then Go (`internal/export/space.go`) prints each page's Document and
Canvas, prints the contents (`contents.go`) with the sheets those start
on, again if the contents' own length moves them (at most three times),
fills and numbers every sheet from the contents on, joins them and adds
bookmarks, folders over their pages. `PagePDF` now shares those steps.

**Deviations**
- **`SpacePDF` returns `SpacePDFResult`** (`error`, `code`, `page`), not
  `Problem`, so a failing page comes back by its path.
- **What a failure says** (decision 14, "which page and why"): a page that
  cannot be read, "Launch plan could not be read, so no PDF was made."; a
  page the system cannot print, the same with Go's reason after it.
- **A page whose canvas block cannot be read still prints its words**, in
  both exports, as opening it does.
- **Changing What while pages are drawn stops the drawing**; it was for the
  folder chosen before.
- **The contents have no paper colour of their own**: the fill under every
  sheet gives it.
- **Not done, with reasons:** no test of `PdfOptions` alone (its settings
  are bound, so it is tested through both dialogs, the unit conversion
  included); the Cancel test still waits a fixed 1.5 s after speeding the
  remaining reads up (a drawing that went on would print well within it;
  breaking Cancel on purpose made the test fail).

**Red first:** contents, Space PDF (build failure, then behaviour),
bound method, `spaceEntries`, both dialogs' new tests, the failure
messages, the contents limit (four prints for three) were seen failing
first; `PdfOptions` was a move, kept green by the existing dialog tests.
