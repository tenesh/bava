# 15.2c: A page as a PDF

**Goal:** Export page offers PDF beside the Markdown zip: the page's
Document, its Canvas, or both, printed by the system's own webview at a
chosen paper, orientation and margins, light or dark, with page numbers if
asked.
**Specs:** `.claude/work/specs/export.md` decisions 1 to 6, 8 and 9
(2026-10-11); `.claude/work/specs/documents.md` decision 48;
`.claude/work/plans/15.2a-pdf-proof.md` (As built, and what it left for
15.2c); `docs/ipc.md`; `.ai/rules/design-system.md`, `ipc.md`,
`editors.md`, `canvas.md`, `wails.md`, `testing.md`.
**File format impact:** none. A PDF is a copy written where the user
chooses; nothing new is written into a Space.
**UI impact:** reused: `PageExportDialog` (gains Format), `Segments`,
`Select`, `Toggle`, `Dialog`; added: `NumberField` (wrapping Ark's
NumberInput, for Custom paper and margins) and `PdfPreview` (the first
sheet). Strings in `messages.ts`; tokens added first if any are needed.

## Constraints

- **Each system's own page printing** (decision 1): `internal/pdfprint`,
  proven on all three in 15.2a. Real, selectable text, drawn as Bava draws
  the page. Nothing leaves the machine: the printing webview loads one
  local file and nothing else.
- **Paper** (decision 3): A3, A4, A5, B4, B5, Letter, Legal, Tabloid and
  Custom (width and height in millimetres or inches); first offered by
  region, Letter in the US and Canada, A4 elsewhere.
- **Margins** (decision 4): None, Narrow, Normal, Wide (0, 12, 20, 30 mm),
  Normal the default, and Custom (top, bottom, left, right).
- **The Canvas on its own PDF page, fitted** (decision 5): after the
  Document, scaled down to fit whole (never enlarged), on a landscape page
  when wider than tall.
- **Page numbers** (decision 8): "3", bottom centre, Bava's font.
- **Fit to width** (decision 9): no scale; the page's width in Bava fills
  the paper between the margins.
- **Go owns the files** (`ipc.md`): it reads the pictures and fonts a page
  shows, prints and writes; errors are data with a `code`; the root is
  checked against the folders opened this session.

### Settled in this plan (say if you disagree)

1. **15.2c is a page's PDF; a folder's or the Space's PDF is 15.2d**
   (one combined file with contents and bookmarks, or one per page). The
   page proves the printing of real pages (fonts, pictures, dark, page
   numbers, a canvas page) on all three systems before many pages are
   joined. Until then Export Space stays the zip alone.
2. **The page is printed from the editor that shows it**, read-only and
   out of sight, so equations, code, tables and callouts print exactly as
   they look; the block handle, copy buttons and other controls are
   hidden. Its styles go with it, and Go puts the fonts and pictures
   inside the file it prints.
3. **What is not on disk prints as its link**: a picture from the web, an
   online video, a link card. The printing webview never reaches the
   network. A video file prints as its file card (name and size); a
   canvas embed as the picture it already shows.
4. **Dark is dark to the paper's edge**: the margins are filled too, not
   left white.
5. **A full-width page** is laid out at the paper's width between the
   margins, as it would be in a window that wide.
6. **Include** offers Document, Canvas, or Both; the choice starts at the
   view the page is open in (Both when it is Both). A page with nothing on
   its Canvas offers no choice and prints its Document.
7. **The preview is the first sheet** as the app lays it out at the chosen
   paper, margins and theme, drawn in the dialog; it is not a printed PDF,
   which takes too long to redo at each change.
8. **The file is chosen with the system's save dialog**, named after the
   page (`Launch plan.pdf`), as the zip is. A loose page needs no Space for
   a PDF: its folder is already opened for its pictures.
9. **Joining the parts and adding numbers and dark margins** is done after
   printing, with pdfcpu (Apache-2.0, a Go library; nothing written to its
   config folder). Page numbers are a page printed by the same webview in
   Bava's font and laid over each sheet, so they never fall back to a
   system font.

### To raise: the milestone's exit criterion

The roadmap's exit criterion asks for "an exported diagram byte-identical
to the `Render` output". Diagram from Code no longer keeps its D2: it
becomes ordinary shapes and arrows, which export through the canvas's own
drawing. There is no diagram element left to compare. I propose striking
that clause and keeping the Space round trip (export and import) for 15.3.

## As built (2026-10-11)

Export page offers Format, PDF or Markdown zip. A PDF is the page drawn in
a read-only editor out of sight (`docs/print-page.ts`), its Canvas drawn by
the canvas's own export (`canvas/export/page-picture.ts`), made into one
self-contained file in Go (`internal/export`: fonts and pictures inside,
a policy that lets it load nothing, the page zoomed to fill the paper's
width), printed by `internal/pdfprint`, which now loads the page from a
temporary file on every system. The Canvas prints on a sheet of its own;
dark sheets are filled to the edge and numbers laid over them after
printing with pdfcpu (stateless: nothing written to its settings
folder); the parts are joined and written like a save. The dialog
previews the first sheet in a sandboxed frame.

**Deviations**
- **A page outside a Space exports a PDF without opening its folder as a
  Space** (settled 8); its dialog offers no zip. 15.2b's "Export works on a
  Space" question is gone for export, and its string with it.
- **The preview's frame document is built in `files/pdf-preview.ts`**, not
  the component: a `<style>` inside a string in a Svelte script is read by
  the style preprocessor as a real one.
- **Segments carry `data-value`**, so the end-to-end walk can click a
  choice by its value.
- **Page numbers need their colour from the app**: Go has no fallback.
- **Tests written with their code in one step**: `page-picture.ts` (then
  seen failing by breaking it on purpose) and `fileURL`'s move; the
  pdfprint temporary-file, e2e heavy-sample, `export` and `pdffile` tests
  were seen failing first.
- **The `pdffile` check reads sheets and sizes, not words**: a printed
  PDF's text is in embedded, subset fonts, so finding a word means
  decoding each font's glyphs. The plan's "whether a word is in its text"
  is dropped; the PDFs kept with the run's downloads are read by eye.
- **pdfcpu nudged `golang.org/x/image`, `x/text`, `x/net` and `x/sys`**;
  the D2 goldens were rerun green after.

**Review** (spec-reviewer): one blocker, fixed: the dialog read its paper
when it opened, before the page was drawn, so it always offered A4 and
never Letter in the US or Canada; the paper is now given at once (a test
