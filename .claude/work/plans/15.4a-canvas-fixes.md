# 15.4a: Zoom to fit, drags the system ends, labels that fit

**Goal:** fit the view to the drawing or the selection, never leave a drag
stuck when the system cuts it off, and never hide a shape's label.
**Specs:** `.claude/work/specs/canvas-work.md` decisions 1 to 3;
`.claude/plan/roadmap.md` 15.4; `.ai/rules/canvas.md`.
**File format impact:** no new keys. A shape's `h` now grows to hold its
label when the label, the shape's size or the label's font size changes;
`docs/file-format.md` says so where it describes `label`.
**UI impact:** two View menu items (drawn as the mockup's "4.9 Zoom to
fit"); labels laid out as "4.8 A label longer than its shape".

## Constraints

- "A label longer than its shape makes the shape taller ... resizing it
  smaller stops at the label's height. Its width stays the user's. In every
  shape the label keeps clear of curved and slanted edges."
- "A word wider than its shape breaks across lines ... Nothing spills past
  the sides."
- "View ▸ Zoom to Fit (`⇧1`) and Zoom to Selection (`⇧2`) zoom in or out,
  as far as it takes, to show what they fit with a margin, centred, within
  the canvas's limits (10% to 800%)."
- A drag the system cuts off "ends as a release does" (roadmap).
- One line-breaker for the stage and the SVG export (`.ai/rules/canvas.md`).
- Undo stays one step per gesture (`history.mutate`).

## How it works

- **The label's box** (`canvas/label-box.ts`, new): for each shape kind, the
  rectangle inside it where a label may sit, from the shape's `w` and `h`:
  a rectangle inset at the sides as today; an ellipse its inscribed box
  (`w/√2` by `h/√2`); a diamond its middle half; a hexagon or
  parallelogram less its slants; a cylinder below its top cap; a document
  above its wave; a cloud its middle; a person its body, below the head. Each is linear in `h`, so the height a label needs inverts exactly:
  `neededHeight(kind, w, textHeight)`. The stage, the label editor and the
  SVG export all place labels by it.
- **Breaking long words:** `wrapLines` gains a `breakWords` option: a word
  wider than the line is split at the last character that fits. Labels
  use it; text elements keep their own rule.
- **Growing:** wherever a label, a shape's width or its label's font size is
  changed (the label editor's commit, resize, the style panel, paste of a
  style), the same `history.mutate` sets `h` to at least `neededHeight`.
  Resize clamps `h` there as the pointer moves. Nothing grows on open: a
  file whose label needs more room on this system than where it was saved
  draws its last lines past the shape's bottom (never dropped) until the
  label or the shape is next changed.
- **Fit:** `viewport.fit(box, size, margin)` picks the zoom that shows `box`
  inside `size` less the margin, within the minimum and maximum zoom, and
  centres it. `view.zoomToFit` fits `drawnBoundsOf` every element;
  `view.zoomToSelection` the selection's. With nothing to show, nothing
  moves.
- **A drag cut off:** `pointercancel` and `lostpointercapture` on the canvas,
  and the window losing focus, end a drag under way through the same path
  as `pointerup`, at the last point the drag reached. Nothing is left set.

## As built

## As built (2026-10-12)

A shape's label sits in the box inside its outline (`canvas/label-box.ts`:
`labelArea`, `placeLabel`), placed there alike by the stage, the SVG export
and the label editor; a word too wide for its line breaks across lines
(`wrapLines` with `breakWords`), as the editor's field does
(`overflow-wrap: anywhere`). Committing a label, a font-size change, pasting
a style and a resize run `growToLabel` in the same undo step, so the shape is
always tall enough; a resize keeps the edge not being dragged. A label taller
than its stored shape (a file saved where text measured narrower) draws on
past the bottom, never cut. View ▸ Zoom to Fit (⇧1) and Zoom to Selection (⇧2)
fit with `--size-fit-margin`, in or out within 10% to 800%. A drag cut off by
`pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture` or the window losing focus ends through
the same `finish` step a release takes.

**Deviations**
- **Decision 4 was added mid-build.** The fixture's diamond showed a short
  word breaking in a diamond's middle half; drawn as the mockup's "4.10", the
  user kept the middle half. The same applies to every shape: small
  ellipses break short words too (the colours sheet's "gree / n").
- **Decision 3 changed after the plan was written**: fitting fills the view
  (up to 800%), no longer stopping at 100%.
- **`neededHeight` searches** rather than inverting a formula: some areas
  stop growing with the height (a slant capped at its share of the width),
  and `labelHeight` breaks the label again at each height found.
- **Rectangles and frames now inset their label on every side**, as the
  export already did; the stage inset only the sides.
- **Long labels in old files run on past their shapes** where they were cut
  off before (the `labels--long` sheet); each grows when edited.
- **Zoom to Fit and Zoom to Selection are canvas-scoped shortcuts**, not
  native accelerators: Shift+1 is "!" in a text field.
- The SVG fixture `export/__fixtures__/scene.svg` was regenerated and read:
  labels move into their areas, and the diamond's word breaks.
