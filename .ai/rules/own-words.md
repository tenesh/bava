# Own words

How the repository talks about other products: it doesn't.

## Never name another product as a comparison or a source
Specs, plans, rules, comments, tests and commits say what Bava does and why,
in its own words; never "as another app does", never a study of one kept here.
Review checks it.

## What stays named
`NOTICE`, where a licence requires the source of adapted code. Also what Bava
is built on: dependencies, operating systems, webviews, stores, formats and
standards (Markdown, D2, Mermaid, TeX), and GitHub. Interoperability goes
unbranded: "other Markdown editors", "a spreadsheet's copied cells".

## Looking elsewhere is fine; keeping it is not
Learning how something behaves elsewhere, in conversation, is fine. Only the
decision and Bava's reason for it reach the repository.
