# AI disclosure

What Claude may do around the store submission.

## Claude never touches the Flathub manifest or submission
Flathub bans AI-assisted manifests and AI-written submission PRs, commits or
replies. Claude writes, edits, reviews and suggests nothing for
`com.tharabyte.bava.*` (here or in Flathub's repo); the maintainer does it.
Breaking this risks a permanent ban. Settings deny the edit; a shell write is
just as forbidden.

## Bava's own AI features are the user's
Bava talks only to endpoints the user sets up, with their credentials. When
those features are built, the stores' listings and privacy answers describe
them. An AI disclosure, if a store asks for one, is written at release.
